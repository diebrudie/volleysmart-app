Past Events — layout proposals

Two alternatives to the current table-with-lines. Both drop the column rules, give each event its own card, and read the result at a glance via a colored chip. Pick one (or mix), and it becomes the new PastEventsTable.

Option A

Date pill + result chip

Roomy cards with a tinted calendar pill on the left, title/club in the middle, and a result chip on the right. A thin colored accent bar signals win / loss / cancelled without reading the numbers.
